01 Who we are

ExpenceTracker ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal finance application for Android that helps you record income and expenses, plan budgets, track goals, loans, subscriptions, bills and reminders, and view insights about your money.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, why we collect it, how it is protected, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

02 Information we collect

a. Information you provide

  • Account information — your name and email address when you create an account, either with email and password or with Google Sign-In. If you use Google Sign-In, we receive only your basic profile (name and email address) from Google.
  • Optional personal details — for example phone number, date of birth, gender, occupation or city, if you choose to add them to your profile. These are optional and can be edited or cleared at any time.
  • Financial information you enter — the accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, loans, subscriptions, bills, categories and reminders that you create in the app. This information is provided by you and used only to operate the app's features.

b. Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical information — such as app version, device platform and language, used to keep the app working correctly on your device.
  • Sync status — whether your device is connected to the internet, so the app knows when it can synchronise your data.

The app does not use advertising identifiers and does not include third-party advertising or behavioural tracking.

c. Device permissions

Permission Why it is used
Notifications To deliver the reminders and alarms you schedule in the app. Notifications are generated on your device.
Biometrics / Fingerprint To lock the app when you enable the optional App Lock. Your biometric data is verified by your device's operating system; the app never sees or stores it.
Internet access To sign in and to synchronise your data with your account when you are online.

03 How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To provide the app's features — recording transactions, budgets, goals, insights, reminders and reports.
  • To synchronise your data across sessions and devices when you are signed in and online.
  • To send you the reminders and notifications you have scheduled.
  • To protect the app and your data, and to respond to your support requests.

We do not use your financial information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the app for you. We do not sell your personal information.

04 Where your data is stored

On your device first. ExpenceTracker is designed to work offline. Your data is stored in a local database on your device, so you can use the app without an internet connection.

In your account (cloud sync). When you are signed in and online, your data is synchronised to our cloud database provider, Supabase, so your information is available to you across sessions and devices. Cloud data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and access is restricted so that each account can only access its own data.

If you sign in with Google, Google processes your sign-in as described in Google's own privacy policy. We only receive your name and email address for the purpose of creating and identifying your account.

05 Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers that make the app work:

  • Supabase — hosting for authentication and data synchronisation.
  • Google — only if you choose Google Sign-In, to authenticate you.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of users or the public.

06 Retention & deletion

  • Your data is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • You can edit or remove most information directly in the app at any time (for example, accounts, transactions, and personal details).
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.
  • You can request deletion of your account and all cloud data by contacting us at the email below. We will process such requests within a reasonable period (generally within 30 days).

07 Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to access, correct, export or delete it, and to object to certain processing. Within the app you can:

  • View and edit your personal details (Profile → Personal Information).
  • Export your data as CSV from the Reports section.
  • Delete individual records at any time.

For anything else — including full account deletion — contact us at the email below and we will help.

08 Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS), access controls on cloud data, and an optional fingerprint app lock on your device. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

09 Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10 International processing

Our service providers may store and process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we take steps to ensure your information receives an adequate level of protection consistent with this policy.

11 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where the changes are significant, provide notice in the app. Continued use of the app after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
